When to call an emergency plumber and when it can wait.

An emergency plumber is the right call when water is spreading, sewage is backing up, a pipe has burst or hot water and heat have stopped in cold weather. These problems damage floors, walls and belongings within minutes or hours, so waiting for a daytime appointment costs more.

Other problems can wait for the next available slot, such as a slow drain in one sink, a dripping faucet or a toilet that runs. Not sure which category you are in? Call and describe what you see. The dispatcher will tell you honestly whether it needs help tonight.

Call now.

Water spreading, a burst pipe, sewage in a drain, a leaking tank or a flooding basement. See burst pipe repair.

Call tonight.

No hot water in winter, a backed up main drain or no heat from a boiler. See drain cleaning.

Book a visit.

A dripping faucet, a running toilet or a planned remodel. See faucet repair.

Gas smell.

Leave the house and call 911 and the gas utility from outside. Then call us once it is safe.

What we promise and what we do not.

We promise to answer the phone around the clock, to send a plumber as soon as one is available and to give you a clear price before repair work starts. We do not promise a specific arrival minute, because traffic, weather and call volume change it, and we will not pretend otherwise.

We also do not invent reviews, awards or years in business. According to the Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection, anyone can check a plumber's license through its eLicense lookup, and you can use it for us or for anyone else you are weighing. Honest information is the best reason to call us again.
