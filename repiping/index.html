Repiping in Stratford, CT.

Repiping replaces the water supply pipes inside a home, from the main shutoff to the fixtures. It differs from fixing a single leak because it replaces the aging system rather than one broken section.

This is a planned project. If a pipe has burst right now, shut the main and see our burst pipe repair page, then decide on repiping once the water is under control.

Decision signs.

Signs it is time to repipe.

Leaks in different places.

A second and third leak within a few years points to pipe that is failing everywhere.

Rusty or brown water.

Corroded galvanized steel releases rust that stains fixtures and laundry.

Low water pressure at every tap.

Mineral and rust buildup narrows older galvanized pipe from the inside.

Pinhole leaks in copper.

Small blue-green stains on copper often mean thin walls are failing.

Known problem materials.

Polybutylene piping, common in homes built roughly from the late 1970s to mid 1990s, has a poor failure record.

Water tastes metallic.

Metal ions from corroding pipe can change taste and color.

Copper or PEX: how the two compare.

Neither is the single right answer. We explain which fits your house, budget and preferences and note that a code-approved material list applies. Ask the Stratford building department about permits for a repipe.

Factor.Copper.PEX.
Standard.Copper tube commonly follows ASTM B88.PEX tube commonly follows ASTM F876.
Installation.Soldered or press fittings, more labor.Flexible runs and fewer joints, faster in walls.
Freeze behavior.Rigid, can split when frozen.Flexible, tolerates some freezing but is not freeze-proof.
Sunlight and outdoor use.Fine outdoors.Not for exposed sunlight.
Cost.Usually higher in material and labor.Usually lower overall.

What a repipe does and does not replace.

A repipe covers the supply lines inside the house. It does not automatically include the buried service line from the street, the drain lines or the fixtures. If your service line is old, ask Aquarion about it. Aquarion's 2025 report says homes built before 1986 may have lead service lines, so check its inventory at aquarionwater.com/lead.

Drains are a separate matter. If they are cast iron and failing, see our sewer line repair page for how that is handled.

Local note.

Repiping older Stratford homes.

If your Stratford house was built before about 1960, ask whether it still has galvanized steel supply lines, and check under sinks and in the basement for gray threaded pipe. Homes that have had pipes partly updated over the years can end up with a mix of materials.

Mixed materials can corrode where they meet. When we plan a repipe, we aim to remove the old runs rather than connecting new pipe to a corroded section.

According to the International Code Council, Connecticut's state building codes are based on the 2021 International Codes, so ask the Stratford building department which edition applies to your project.

Owners of older buildings can read our Stratford Center guide on inspections and historic district questions.
