ZIP codes at a glance.

Stratford uses ZIP codes 06614 and 06615. Our town pages list the ZIP codes we serve in each place, including 06604, 06605, 06606 and 06610 in Bridgeport, 06460 and 06461 in Milford, 06611 in Trumbull, 06484 in Shelton and 06824, 06825 and 06890 in Fairfield. Give the dispatcher your full address and ZIP, and the call is routed from there.

How dispatch works from Stratford.

When you call, the dispatcher asks for your address and ZIP code and matches the call to the soonest available plumber, not to a fixed territory. A plumber already working in Bridgeport may take a Stratford call, and a plumber finishing in Stratford may head to Trumbull or Milford. That flexibility is why the 24/7 line is one number for all of these places.

Bridges and highways decide most of the timing. The Housatonic bridges to Milford and the I-95 corridor toward Bridgeport and Fairfield can slow during rush hour or after a storm, and the dispatcher builds that into the window.

Smaller places we also reach.

Some communities are too small for a page of their own, but the same 24/7 line covers them. Devon is a village in Milford. Easton and Monroe lie north of Trumbull. Huntington is a historic section of Shelton, and Southport is a village within Fairfield. Travel to the farther places takes longer, and the dispatcher gives an honest window.

Roads and landmarks that shape dispatch.

Interstate 95 and US Route 1 along the Boston Post Road are the main ways between Stratford, Bridgeport and Fairfield, and the Merritt Parkway crosses northern Stratford toward Trumbull. The Housatonic River forms the eastern boundary with Milford, so the river bridges decide how fast we reach that side.

Water service in this part of Fairfield County comes largely from Aquarion's Eastern Fairfield County system, which supplies Stratford, Bridgeport, Fairfield, Shelton and Trumbull according to its 2025 water quality report. Sewer service and building permits are handled by each town, so ask your own town's offices about those.

Sikorsky Memorial Airport sits in Stratford and is owned by Bridgeport, which is a good reminder that town lines and plumbing districts do not always match. If you are not sure whether your address falls in our range, call and a dispatcher will confirm.
