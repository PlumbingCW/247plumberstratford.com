A 24/7 plumbing line for Stratford and nearby towns.

Stratford 24/7 Plumbing is a plumbing dispatch service for homes and small businesses in Stratford, Connecticut. We answer the phone at any hour, take the details of the problem and send a plumber as soon as one is available.

We built the service around the situations where people need help most, which are leaks, backups, hot water failures and storm damage. Our pages explain each problem plainly so you can understand what is going on before anyone arrives.

How we work.

Every call starts with a dispatcher who asks where the problem is, how fast it is changing and whether anyone is at risk. If a gas smell or carbon monoxide alarm is involved, the first instruction is always to leave the building and call 911 from outside.

The plumber who arrives stops the damage first, then finds the cause, then explains the options. You get a clear price before repair work begins. If a repair is not the best answer, the plumber says so, whether that means replacing a water heater or leaving a small problem for a scheduled visit.

Why local knowledge matters.

Plumbing problems follow the age and layout of the house around them, and no two Stratford streets are alike. Shoreline areas deal with groundwater and storms.

Water in Stratford comes from Aquarion's Eastern Fairfield County system. Its 2025 water quality report lists average hardness at about 3 grains per gallon, so scale is a smaller concern here than in harder water areas, and most failures trace back to age and corrosion. That kind of local detail shapes how we diagnose a call.

What we cover.

We offer 21 services, including drain cleaning, water heater repair, leak detection, burst pipe repair and sewer line repair. We also handle planned work such as repiping and plumbing installation.

Our service area includes Stratford and the nearby towns of Bridgeport, Milford, Trumbull, Shelton and Fairfield. See the full service area page for neighborhoods and landmarks.

What a typical emergency call looks like.

Picture a basement floor drain that is gurgling at midnight with brown water rising. The dispatcher asks whether other fixtures are affected, tells the caller to stop running water and shut off power near the wet area and books the soonest available plumber. On arrival, the plumber checks the main line, clears it and runs a camera if the cause is not obvious.

The same pattern holds for a leaking tank or a frozen pipe in a cold garage. Stop the damage, find the cause, explain the options and give a clear price before repairs begin. Call to ask about timing for problems that are urgent but not an active flood.

What we do not do.

We do not post reviews we did not receive, and we do not claim awards or a number of years in business we cannot back up. We also do not make up prices, since the right price depends on the problem. When a plumber has seen the problem, you get a clear figure before repairs begin.

Planned work gets the same care.

Not every call is an emergency. Remodels, new fixtures, water heater swaps and basement drainage are scheduled with a walk-through and a clear price. The same 24/7 line takes those requests, and the dispatcher will tell you when a visit can be arranged.

Check us and check anyone else.

Before you hire any plumber, verify the license. According to the Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection, its eLicense lookup lets you search by name and confirm the status. Ask for a clear price, ask what the plumber found and ask what the alternatives are. A good company welcomes those questions.
