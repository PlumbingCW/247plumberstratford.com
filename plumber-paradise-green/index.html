Neighborhood guide.

Where Paradise Green is.

Paradise Green is a neighborhood and retail center in Stratford in ZIP code 06614. Neighborhood data places three main roads here: Main Street (Route 113), Nichols Avenue (Route 108) and Barnum Avenue (US 1). Local streets include North Avenue, Freeman Avenue, Hillside Avenue, Park Street and King Street. The area covers a little under three quarters of a square mile, and a small stream called Long Brook runs through it.

Commercial real estate listings advertise retail space in Paradise Green, and home listings show many properties changing hands. Public sources do not give an official boundary, so we describe the area by its main roads.
