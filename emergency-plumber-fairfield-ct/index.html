Top plumbing calls in Fairfield, CT.

Fairfield homes tend to be larger and more finished than the typical house in neighboring towns, with multiple bathrooms, kitchens updated for custom fixtures and finished basements. When something fails, the damage costs more, so speed matters. The 24/7 line is open around the clock.

The first calls we expect from Fairfield are hidden leaks and high water bills, water heaters in homes with more than one tank, clogged main drains and leaking bathroom fixtures.

Water damage in a Fairfield home.

In a finished house, a slow drip behind drywall can turn into a ceiling collapse within hours. Close the main shutoff, turn off power to the area and call the 24/7 line.

If water is on a stairway or near electrical panels, stay clear and tell the dispatcher so the plumber arrives prepared.
