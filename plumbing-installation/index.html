Plumbing installation in Stratford, CT.

Plumbing installation is planned work: adding or moving a sink, toilet, tub, shower, laundry hookup or kitchen. It includes supply lines, drains, vents, shutoff valves and the final fixture connection.

This page is for planned projects. If water is leaking or a drain has backed up, call the 24/7 line instead, and see pages such as drain cleaning or burst pipe repair.

Rough-in, inspection and closing the wall.

Rough-in is the stage where pipes are in the walls and floors but fixtures are not yet set. It is the best time for inspection and pressure testing, since a leak found now is far cheaper than one found behind finished tile.

Permit and inspection requirements vary by town and project. Ask the Stratford building department what applies. The Office of the Building Official lists an online permitting portal for applications. You can also confirm a plumber's license through Connecticut's eLicense lookup.

Planning the order of work in a remodel.

The plumber usually works in two visits, a rough-in before the walls are closed and a finish visit once tile, cabinets and countertops are in place, and the schedule matters because a delay in one trade can push the other back by days. Tell us early which fixtures you have already bought, since the rough-in dimensions depend on the exact model you choose and a change after the walls close is expensive to fix.

If the contractor running your remodel needs the plumber on site on a certain day, say so when you call, and the dispatcher will tell you honestly whether that date can be held before you commit to a schedule that depends on it.
