What the guides cover.

The guides fall into a few groups. Drain and toilet guides explain clogs, plungers and what works. Water heater guides help you decide between repair, replacement and tankless. Pipe and basement guides cover bursts, sump pumps and waterproofing, and a gas and boiler pair covers heating safety.

How to use these guides.

Start with the guide that matches your symptom. If you smell gas, see sewage or have water spreading, do not read first. Leave the building if there is a gas smell, or shut off the main water valve if water is spreading, then call.

The guides are general information. A plumber who can see your pipes can tell you more than any article, so use them to ask better questions and not as a substitute for a visit.
