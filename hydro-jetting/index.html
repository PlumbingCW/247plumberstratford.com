Hydro jetting in Stratford, CT.

Hydro jetting uses a pressurized water stream from a nozzle that travels through the line, scouring grease, sludge, scale and root hairs from the full circumference of the pipe. Water is the only cleaning agent, so there are no chemicals going into the sewer.

When a sewer line is backing up at night, the 24/7 line is the place to start. Call to ask about timing. Until the plumber arrives, stop using water, keep children and pets away from backed-up floor drains and move belongings off the floor.

Act now.

Sewer backup: what to do before jetting.

Jetting is often the right tool for a main sewer line that backs up repeatedly or a restaurant-style grease blockage. If sewage is entering the house, treat it as an emergency, because it creates a health hazard and can ruin flooring within hours.

Do not run the washing machine or dishwasher, do not flush, and do not try to push the blockage with a hose or chemical product. Call the 24/7 line and describe which drains are affected.

Jetting compared with cable cleaning.

Our drain cleaning page covers lighter fixes. When a cable keeps getting stuck on roots or a hard scale layer, jetting is the next step.

Why we inspect first.

Water at jetting pressure can find weaknesses in a pipe. A camera pass before the work shows whether the line is round and sound or cracked, offset or partly collapsed. If the pipe is damaged, we stop and explain repair options instead of forcing water through a failing joint.

We run the camera again after jetting to show you the cleaned pipe, which is also useful evidence if you are planning sewer line repair later.

Uses.

Where jetting fits best.

Main sewer lines with repeated backups.

If a cable only buys a few weeks, jetting removes the buildup the cable leaves behind.

Kitchen lines carrying grease.

Grease hardens on cool pipe walls. Hot, pressurized water cuts it far better than a cable head.

Older iron and clay lines with scale.

Rough interiors catch debris. Jetting smooths the pipe surface, provided the pipe is sound.

Root hairs in a lateral.

Light roots can be cleared, and a root cutter may be needed first for heavy growth.

Floor drains and cleanouts.

Basement and garage drains can be jetted through the cleanout to reach the main.

Preventive cleaning.

A scheduled jetting can reset a line that clogs more than once a year.

Local note.

Jetting and Stratford's older lines.

Tree roots are a frequent cause of line buildup wherever mature trees grow near an older sewer line. If a camera shows roots entering at joints, jetting alone gives temporary relief, and a longer-term repair may be the better plan.

Stratford's Water Pollution Control office is the right contact if you are unsure where the private line ends and the town main begins. Ask before any excavation.

The national 811 number connects you to the utility locating service, and a locate request before digging is the standard first step for any buried line work.
