Condominium guide.

What Oronoque Village is.

Oronoque Village is a gated condominium community in north Stratford for residents who are 55 or older. Listings describe more than 900 homes built in the 1970s and arranged two to a building, each pair sharing one wall. The homes range from about 1,420 to 2,800 square feet. The community is built around a golf course and sits near the Merritt Parkway, the Milford Connector and Interstate 95.

Listings also note that the utilities were placed underground during development. We have no information about the plumbing inside any home, and age alone does not tell you what is installed. What this page can do is explain how to sort out who owns a problem and how to get it fixed.

When something leaks, work through these four steps.

1.

Stop the water.

Close the shutoff for the fixture, or the main valve for the unit, and move belongings away from the wet area.

2.

Tell the shared-wall neighbor.

If water is near the common wall, let the other household know so they can check their side.

3.

Contact the association office.

Ask whether the problem is the owner's or the association's to repair, and whether contractors must be approved.

4.

Call a plumber.

Say that it is a condominium and describe where the water is coming from.

Water heaters.

Water heaters and the 120 degree setting.

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission recommends setting a water heater to 120 degrees Fahrenheit to reduce the risk of tap water scalds. That matters in any home, and it is easy to check and adjust. If your tank is leaking, makes popping sounds or no longer keeps up, see water heater replacement or water heater repair and ask the plumber to check the thermostat setting.

Because many residents are older adults, we suggest asking for a heater location you can reach and a shutoff you can turn without kneeling or lifting.

Water heater or shutoff trouble?

Ask about a safe thermostat setting and a shutoff you can reach.

Local note.

Water, sewer and digging.

Water service in Stratford comes from Aquarion. Whether your drain lines are part of the town sewer system is answered by your association and by Stratford Water Pollution Control, not by a plumber guessing. Even a small digging job needs a call to 811 first.
