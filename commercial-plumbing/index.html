Commercial plumbing in Stratford, CT, for small and mid-size properties.

Commercial plumbing covers the pipes, fixtures and drains of businesses, from a corner shop to a restaurant or a small office building. The scale is bigger than a home, the fixtures see more use and downtime costs real money.

The 24/7 line takes after-hours emergencies. If water is spilling, shut off the nearest isolation valve or the main, protect inventory and electronics and tell the dispatcher the type of business and building access.

After-hours emergencies: what to do first.

Restroom backups, a failed water heater, a burst supply line or a flooded kitchen floor can close a business until the plumbing is working. Call at once and tell us about hours, who has keys and whether a landlord or property manager controls the main shutoff.

Documentation helps. Photograph the damage, note the time and keep any failed parts. Your landlord, tenant agreement and insurer may all ask for the same details.

Backflow testing for commercial water service.

A backflow preventer is a device that keeps water in your building from flowing back into the public supply. Many commercial properties, and any building with irrigation or a fire line, must have it tested on a schedule set by the water utility or local authority.

Testing frequency and who may perform it vary, so ask Aquarion or the Stratford building department what applies to your property. We can arrange testing and repair of a failed device, and the paperwork goes to the party that requires it.

Grease traps for restaurants and food service.

A grease trap or interceptor catches fats, oils and grease before they reach the sewer. A full trap sends grease downstream, where it hardens and can block your line or the town's. Service intervals depend on kitchen volume and the rules that apply to your property.

We pair trap cleaning with a drain line check, because a trap that fills in weeks may point to a kitchen line problem. See our drain cleaning page for how kitchen lines are cleared, and our hydro jetting page for grease-coated lines.

Storm drains and parking-lot catch basins.

Storm drains carry rain away from lots and loading areas. Leaves, silt and trash can clog them, and standing water then enters doors or freezes on the pavement. Cleaning a catch basin or storm line is routine maintenance that is easy to skip until the first heavy rain.

Near the Housatonic River and along the shoreline, heavy rain and high tides can already stress drainage. A clean storm drain before the wet season is cheap protection.

Commercial leak detection: what to expect.

Finding a hidden leak in a commercial building takes methodical isolation of zones, meter checks and listening gear. Results depend on access, pipe layout and shared systems. We will be direct about what is practical and give you written notes.

If you manage several units, tell us who is responsible for which lines so any repair is billed to the right party. Our leak detection page explains the tools in detail.

According to the International Code Council, Connecticut's state building codes are based on the 2021 International Codes, so ask the Stratford building department which edition applies to your project.
