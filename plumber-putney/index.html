Neighborhood guide.

Finding Putney on the map.

Putney is a section in the north end of Stratford. It abuts the Oronoque neighborhood, and its boundaries run roughly from Chapel Street and Harry B. Flood Middle School, across Route 110 and Boothe Memorial Park, to River Road and the Housatonic River. Those edges are informal, so if you are unsure whether your address counts as Putney, that does not change how a plumber helps you.

Landmarks include Boothe Memorial Park, a parkland of about 30 acres with the original Boothe homestead and a collection of historic buildings, and the Putney Chapel, built around 1844.

Common calls.

Putney plumbing questions we can help with.

Water service and meter questions.

A wet spot in the lawn near the meter box or a sudden jump in your bill. See water line repair.

Hidden leaks.

A hissing sound, a spinning meter with everything off or a damp wall. See leak detection.

Water heaters.

Rust-colored water, popping sounds or no hot water. See water heater replacement.

Slow or backing-up drains.

A line near large trees can pick up roots. A camera shows whether that is the cause. See drain cleaning.

Cold-weather pipes.

Additions, garages and exterior walls leave some lines in the coldest part of the house.

Park-side and river-side lots.

Ask the plumber about drainage and the sump pump if a low spot floods.

Local note.

Lead service lines and water in the north end.

Aquarion publishes a lead service line inventory at aquarionwater.com/lead. If you want to know what your water service line is made of, start there instead of guessing from the age of the house. Aquarion's 2025 report lists the Eastern Fairfield County system at about 3 grains per gallon of hardness, which is soft.

For sewer or septic, ask Stratford Water Pollution Control about your address. Call 811 before you dig for a new line or a fence post.
