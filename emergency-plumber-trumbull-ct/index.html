Trumbull service area.

Trumbull homes by age, and what fails in each.

Trumbull grew mostly after the middle of the last century, with colonials, capes, raised ranches and larger newer homes spread across areas such as Trumbull Center, Long Hill and Nichols. That timeline matters, because the age of a house predicts which plumbing parts wear out. We answer emergency calls around the clock.

A house built in the 1950s or 1960s often has its original copper or galvanized lines, a water heater that has been replaced once or twice and drains that started as cast iron or clay. A house from the 1990s on has plastic drains and newer fixtures but may still have a builder-grade water heater near the end of its life.

Act now.

A Trumbull emergency does not wait for morning.

A burst supply line in a finished basement or a tank leaking onto a carpet needs attention within minutes. Close the main, shut off power to the wet area and call the 24/7 line.

If the leak is at the water heater, shut the cold supply valve above the tank, turn off its power or gas and tell the dispatcher what kind of heater you have.

Local note.

Trumbull, Easton and Monroe.

Trumbull has a single ZIP code, 06611. Easton (06612) and Monroe (06468) lie farther north, and calls from them are within reach of our dispatch too, though the trip is longer.

Many Trumbull homes lie within the Aquarion water system that also serves Stratford, so the soft water noted in Aquarion's 2025 report applies, and failures there are usually age related instead of scale related. Ask whether your street has public sewer before assuming a drain problem is a town issue.
